# Tears of the Kingdom Recipe Book

Cooking, fusing, building and a painted map of Hyrule, worked out from your own bag. This folder is the app version: put it on GitHub Pages once, then install it on your Android phone like any other app. It opens full screen from your app drawer and works offline.

## 1. Put it on GitHub (once)

1. Unzip the download first. On a Pixel, open **Files**, tap the zip, then **Extract**.
2. Sign in at **github.com** (a free account is fine).
3. Tap **+** (top right), then **New repository**. Give it a name, for example `recipe-book`, leave it **Public**, and tap **Create repository**.
4. On the new repository's page, tap **uploading an existing file** (or **Add file**, then **Upload files**).
5. Tap **choose your files** and select **all** the files from the unzipped folder at once. They all go at the top level, no folders. Then tap **Commit changes**.
   If you don't see the upload option on your phone, open Chrome's **⋮** menu and tick **Desktop site**.
6. Open **Settings**, then **Pages**. Under **Build and deployment**, set **Source** to **Deploy from a branch**, set **Branch** to **main** and the folder to **/ (root)**, and tap **Save**.
7. Wait a minute or two, then reload the Pages screen until it says **Your site is live at** `https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/recipe-book/`.

## 2. Install it on the phone

1. Open that address in **Chrome** on the phone.
2. Tap **⋮**, then **Install app**. If the menu shows **Add to Home screen** instead, tap that and pick **Install** if Chrome offers it.
3. **Recipe Book** now sits in your app drawer. Open it once while online; after that it works without a connection.

## 3. Bring your bag over

Each copy keeps its own bag. In the copy that has your bag (for example the one in Claude): **Shop**, then the **Bag** tab, **Move or back up your bag**, **Copy bag code**. In the installed app: **Shop**, then the **Bag** tab, **Move or back up your bag**, paste the code, then tap **Load this bag** twice. A copied code also works as a backup.

## Updating later

Upload the new files over the old ones (same names) and commit. The next time you open the app online it loads the new version. If the old one still shows, close the app and open it again.

## Good to know

- Free GitHub Pages needs a public repository, so anyone with the address can open the app. Your bag isn't in these files: it stays on your phone. The page asks search engines not to list it.
- **Photos for Claude** only works in the Claude version. The installed app reads **Switch screenshots** right on the phone, no AI.
- Links to guides and sources open in your browser.
- The phone's Back gesture steps back inside an open popup, then closes it; with no popup open it leaves the app as usual.
